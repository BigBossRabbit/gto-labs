02 — The rule that stops it collapsing
Every arm must stand alone. If every arm needs the other two, it is a story, not a structure.

That one line governs everything below it. An arm that cannot survive on its own revenue is a liability dressed as synergy.

03 — The arms

Three arms. One spine.

Deployment

Forward-deployed engineers

Engineers placed inside client companies, working in the client's real tools from day one — never a demo, never a sandbox. They report to us, always. The moment an engineer reports into client management it becomes staff augmentation and the margin collapses.

Supply

The hardware desk

Spec, source, landed cost, burn-in, provision. The only entirely local arm — no visa, no foreign entity, no contractor law — and it saves money immediately because it is currently done ad hoc.

The Lab

R&D and the agent fleet

Reference builds, model work, and the agents that do the shipping. This is the part that compounds.

Spine

Legal, banking, brand, security

Credentials, contracts, banking rails and the knowledge base. Shared — never a bottleneck.

07 — What we deploy

AI that works where your data already lives.

The cloud model bills you per million tokens and asks your records to leave the country. We build the opposite: intelligence that runs on machines you can point at, in your jurisdiction, in the tools you already use.

On-premise inference

Models on your hardware

Local inference engines on small-form-factor machines — a box in your office, not a region in someone else's cloud. Your documents never leave the building.

Agent fleets

Workers in your real tools

Forward-deployed agents that work inside the software you already run — read-only until you authorise more. Built in your systems, never a demo sandbox.

Knowledge systems

Documents that answer back

Your contracts, filings, and records provisioned into a private, searchable knowledge base — the difference between storing information and being able to use it.

The honesty rule

Real, with care, or hype

Every capability we quote is labelled against what the hardware provably holds. We quote nothing a box cannot carry. If a claim is not proven, it does not go in the proposal.

08 — The Token Factory

Rent the box-month. Not the token.

Per-token pricing punishes the users who trust the system most. The Token Factory inverts it: you rent a dedicated machine in Windhoek for the month — inference, rendering, and batch jobs run on silicon that serves nobody else. Your prompts, your records, your renders: they never leave the box.

Dedicated box-month

The unit of compute

One machine, one client, the whole month. Token allowances inside are fair-use, metered, and visible to you — the same numbers we see.

Renders & batch jobs

Priced per job

Image work, redraws, document pipelines — quoted per job with the output defined before the clock starts.

Capability, stated honestly

The matrix governs

Every model is matched to memory the box actually has. What runs today is quoted today; what needs the next generation of hardware is Phase 2, and we say so.

Measured the way operators measure: time-to-first-token, tokens per second, error rate, tokens per watt-hour. You see the meters; there is nothing to trust on faith.

09 — Try it before you own it

Ninety days on our hardware. Then it is yours — or ours to run.

For practices that cannot justify hardware up front — medical, legal, anyone whose records cannot leave the country. We host your inference for up to ninety days. A fixed integration fee starts the clock; the token meter is visible to you the entire time.

Setup

One fixed integration fee

Tooling, data ingestion, hardening — priced up front, in NAD. It funds the work and proves both sides are serious.

Trial

90 days, hard stop

Hosted, isolated, supported. At day thirty both sides review honestly — is this working, and where is it going.

Convert

Your box, or our operation

Buy the machine — spec'd, sourced, and provisioned by the hardware desk. Or keep it running as a managed deployment. Most clients end up with both.

Isolation is decided before the trial, in writing: regulated data gets a box that runs nothing else, with retention and offboarding stated — export in open formats, deletion on written instruction, certificate of deletion. No sub-processors for inference. That is the part cloud cannot say.
